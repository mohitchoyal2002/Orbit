## Review process

1. Keep raw customer records private. Use aggregate results in the proposed publication copy.
2. Paste the entire exact copy, including attribution, into the workspace's Case studies draft.
3. Ask the authorized client member to read the draft and use its approval control.
4. Check that the current content hash matches the approved hash and permission has not been revoked before any publication.
5. Any edit requires fresh approval. Publication remains a separate explicit action.

## Subscription decision after pilots

Record the repeated request category, distinct affected clients, frequency of the problem, delivery/support hours, willingness to pay and measured outcome. A useful decision gate is several paying clients requesting substantially the same workflow and accepting a repeatable service scope. This is a proposed gate, not evidence already collected. Choose billing and subscription features only after that validation.
